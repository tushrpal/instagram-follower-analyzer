2. What the Service does

The core tracker analyses an Instagram data export in your browser to classify follower relationships. Guest results remain in browser session storage. A signed-in user may explicitly save derived username, profile, category, count, relationship, and timestamp data for history and comparisons; the raw ZIP is not saved. A separate optional feature may connect an eligible Instagram professional account through official OAuth.

One snapshot can show current relationships such as non-followers and mutuals. New or lost followers can only be inferred by comparing two snapshots from different dates, and results are limited by the contents and timing of those exports.
