What Analytics Are NOT Included?

Our tool focuses on follower relationship analytics because that's what's included in Instagram's data export. We cannot provide:

  • Post engagement metrics (likes, comments, saves) — not in the export; use Instagram's built-in Professional Dashboard for this
  • Story views and reach — not in the export; check Instagram Insights inside the app
  • Hashtag performance — not in the export; third-party paid tools can track this via API
  • Profile visit analytics — not in the export; available in Instagram's native analytics for Pro accounts
  • Follower demographics (age, gender, location) — not in the export; Instagram Insights shows aggregated data

Our specialty is what Instagram doesn't tell you: who unfollowed you, who doesn't follow back, and tracking these relationships over time with dated snapshots.
