Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 22, 2026

1. Who operates this service

Instagram Follower Tracker is independently developed and operated by Tushr Pal ("we", "us", or "the service"). It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Instagram or Meta Platforms, Inc.

2. ZIP analysis and guest sessions

Your Instagram export ZIP is opened and analysed in your browser. The raw ZIP is not uploaded to our server. When you use the tracker as a guest, the analysis result remains in your browser's sessionStorage; it is not uploaded as a guest result. Browser session data is normally removed when the relevant tab or browser session ends, and you can also clear it through your browser controls.

A single export describes the relationships present in that snapshot. Identifying new or lost followers requires comparing two snapshots from different dates.

3. Optional accounts and saved analyses

If you create an account, we process your email address and store a password hash rather than your plain-text password. Authentication may use a session cookie and browser local storage to keep you signed in. Local storage is also used for preferences such as theme.

A signed-in user may explicitly choose to save an analysis. Only then is derived analysis data uploaded: Instagram usernames, available profile details, relationship categories, counts, and analysis or relationship timestamps. This supports dated history and snapshot comparisons. The raw Instagram ZIP is not uploaded or retained.

4. Optional Instagram professional-account connection

A separate, optional feature may let eligible professional accounts connect through Instagram's official OAuth process. OAuth is not used for ZIP analysis. If you choose this feature, Instagram may provide an access token, account identifier, professional profile information, and the permissions or professional-account metrics you approve. We use that information only to provide the connected feature, and you can disconnect it.

5. Analytics, advertising, video, email, and logs

  • Google Analytics: may receive page and device information, approximate location derived from IP address, referral information, and interaction events. Google may use cookies or similar technologies.
  • Google AdSense: advertisements on eligible pages may use cookies or similar identifiers to deliver and measure ads, subject to Google settings and applicable consent requirements. This privacy page does not itself load an AdSense script.
  • YouTube: the homepage uses a YouTube privacy-enhanced embed. Loading or playing it may send your IP address, browser information, and interaction data to Google/YouTube and may set storage or cookies under their policies.
  • Brevo: we use Brevo to deliver service emails such as verification, password reset, or other transactional messages. Brevo processes the email address, message delivery details, and related technical events needed to send those messages.
  • Hosting and security logs: our hosting infrastructure may record IP address, user agent, request path, response status, and timestamp for operation, reliability, abuse prevention, and security.

6. Why data is used and shared

We use data to operate the service, authenticate users, provide explicitly saved history or connected professional-account features, send requested service emails, understand site performance, prevent abuse, comply with law, and display or measure advertising. Data is shared with the service providers described above only as needed for those purposes or when legally required. We do not sell raw Instagram ZIP files.

7. Retention and deletion

Guest analysis remains only for the browser session unless you clear it sooner. Saved analyses are retained until you delete the saved session or your account. Account information is retained until account deletion, subject to limited records we may need for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or legal obligations. Connected OAuth data is retained until disconnection, account deletion, or expiry where applicable. Provider and hosting logs are kept according to operational and provider retention schedules.

8. Your choices and rights

You can use guest ZIP analysis without creating an account, decline to save an analysis, clear session or local storage in your browser, manage cookies through browser and Google controls, avoid playing the YouTube embed, disconnect an optional professional-account connection, and delete saved sessions or your account.

Depending on where you live, privacy law may give you rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability, or withdrawal of consent. These rights are not universal and may be subject to exceptions. Submit a request through the contact form; we may need to verify your identity before acting.

9. Security and children

We use safeguards intended to protect account and saved-analysis data, including transport encryption and password hashing. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Instagram Follower Tracker is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the service or legal requirements change. The date above identifies the current version. For privacy questions or requests, use our Contact page. Please do not include an Instagram password or raw export in your message.
