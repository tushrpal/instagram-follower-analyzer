About the project

Follower analysis without handing over your Instagram password

Instagram Follower Tracker is an independent web tool that turns the follower and following lists in your official Instagram data export into understandable relationship categories.

It was created and is operated by Tushr Pal, an independent developer. The service is not affiliated with or endorsed by Instagram or Meta.

How ZIP analysis works

  1. You request a JSON export containing followers and following from Instagram.
  2. You select the ZIP in Instagram Follower Tracker. The raw archive is read and parsed in your browser.
  3. As a guest, the resulting analysis stays in browser sessionStorage and is not uploaded as a guest result.
  4. If you sign in, you can explicitly opt in to save derived usernames, available profile details, categories, counts, relationships, and timestamps. The raw ZIP is not saved.

Optional Instagram OAuth belongs to a separate professional-account feature. It is not needed for export-based follower analysis.

What the results mean

Current relationships

One export can identify mutual followers, accounts you follow that do not follow back, followers you do not follow, and categories present in the export such as pending requests.

Changes over time

Two snapshots from different dates are required to infer new and lost followers. A single current export cannot show who unfollowed between dates.

Results depend on the export files supplied, their dates, Instagram's current file format, and account events such as deletion or renaming. The tracker classifies data; it does not reconstruct activity that is absent from a snapshot.

Privacy choices by design

  • Core ZIP analysis does not require an Instagram password.
  • Guest analysis stays within the browser session.
  • Account history is uploaded only after a signed-in user chooses to save it.
  • The raw ZIP is not uploaded for saved history.
  • Saved sessions and accounts can be deleted.
